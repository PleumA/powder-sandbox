Every material has a rule. Fire needs fuel, water quenches it into steam, lava fuses sand into glass, salt turns water to brine, brine boils back to salt. Plants grow by day, sleep at night, and seed themselves; bugs lay eggs, birds build nests, spiders spin webs, worms tunnel, fish and frogs stay wet, each kind keeps to its own — fish school, birds flock, ants march in columns — and one creature bolting from a predator turns its whole group the same way. Weather, season and heat do the rest.
